SCENE B

场景 B · 承包商现场作业安全

1
项目经理 PM
Our contractors have finished all pre-qualification and pre-job preparations and will start on-site work very soon. Could you walk me through our safety management process before they begin work?
我们的承包商已经完成所有前期资格审核入场准备，马上就要进场作业。请你说明一下开工前的整套安全管理流程？
1
E. H. S. 经理
Before any contractor task starts, they should complete an overall pre-task hazard identification and risk assessment for every upcoming work activities. This is the foundation of all on-site risk control.
在承包商任何作业开始前，他们必须针对即将开展的所有工作，完成整体的危害辨识和风险评估，这是所有现场风险管控的基础。
2
项目经理 PM
After they finish hazard identification and risk evaluation, what do they need to do next?
完成危害辨识和风险评估之后，下一步要做什么？
2
E. H. S. 经理
They need to fill out a Pre-Task Plan (PTP) and get approvals. PTP is also referred to as Work Permit in many companies. This document records the related risks, control measures and other safety requirements for the task.
我们需要填写并审批作业前方案，也就是很多公司说的作业许可证。这份文件会记录作业相关的风险、管控措施和安全要求。
3
项目经理 PM
Besides risk analysis and permit approval, what on-site safety work do they need to conduct?
除了风险分析和许可审批，开工后我们还要落实哪些现场安全工作？
3
E. H. S. 经理
They need to ensure all the safety measures indicated in the PTP are in place before the start of the work. In addition, they should perform routine on-site safety inspections at least every week to check contractor workers' behaviors, PPE compliance and risk control status.
他们需要确保作业开始前，作业方案（PTP）中列明的所有安全措施均落实到位，此外，他们至少每周要开展现场安全巡查，核查承包商作业行为、防护用品佩戴以及风险管控的情况。
4
项目经理 PM
Do they have regular safety meetings for contractor ongoing projects?
针对承包商持续作业的项目，他们有定期安全会议吗？
4
E. H. S. 经理
Yes. They hold safety meetings at least on a weekly basis. We review current safety performance, existing hazards and closure status of the corrective actions.
有的。承包商每周至少要开一次安全会议，复盘审核当前安全表现、现存隐患和整改进度。
5
项目经理 PM
Do they have reward and penalty rules for the contractors on site?
他们针对现场承包商，有没有一套奖惩规则？
5
E. H. S. 经理
Yes. They will give fines penalty for contractor workers for their safety violations.
有的。如果查到安全违规，他们会对承包商工人进行罚款。
6
项目经理 PM
Will these fines be reviewed and confirmed at their weekly project meeting?
这些罚款会在我们每周项目例会中审核确认吗？
6
E. H. S. 经理
Exactly. All violation penalties will be discussed and finalized in the weekly meeting.
没错，所有违规罚款都会在周会上商议并最终确定。
7
项目经理 PM
Then what kind of behaviors can be rewarded for contractors?
那承包商哪些行为可以获得奖励呢？
7
E. H. S. 经理
They will get rewards if they proactively report unsafe acts, unsafe conditions or offer good safety suggestions.
如果他们主动上报不安全行为、现场隐患，或是提出有效的安全建议，就能获得奖励。
8
项目经理 PM
To summarize, the whole process covers pre-task hazard identification and risk assessment, PTP or Work Permit application and approval, ongoing site inspection, weekly safety review meetings, and penalty and reward program, correct?
总结来说，整套流程包括：工作前危害识别和风险评估、PTP 作业许可申请和审批、持续现场巡查、每周安全复盘会议、奖惩与表彰制度，对吗？
8
E. H. S. 经理
Yes, this is the complete and standard contractor safety management process for our site.
没错，这就是我们厂区标准、完整的承包商安全管理流程。
