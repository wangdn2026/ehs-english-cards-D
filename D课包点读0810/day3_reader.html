PHRASES

E. H. S. 固定搭配短语

1
固定搭配
housekeeping
现场整洁
1
例句
Contractors must keep site housekeeping in good order throughout the project period.
承包商在项目周期内必须始终保持现场整洁有序。
2
固定搭配
fall protection
防坠落措施
2
例句
Fall protection must be implemented whenever working at heights above six feet.
凡是在 6 英尺以上高处作业，必须落实防坠落措施。
3
固定搭配
lock out tag out (LOTO)
上锁挂牌
3
例句
All LOTO isolation points must be formally removed and verified before site handover.
场地移交前，所有上锁挂牌能源隔离点必须完成规范解除与复核。
4
固定搭配
Pre-Task Plan (PTP)
作业前方案
4
例句
Verify all Pre-Task Plans and work permits for high-risk work are fully closed out.
核对所有高风险作业的作业前方案与作业许可证全部闭环。
5
固定搭配
toolbox talk
班前安全交底会
5
例句
Daily toolbox talks must be conducted before any contractor shift starts.
承包商每个班次开工前必须召开班前安全交底会。
6
固定搭配
leading indicator
前置指标
6
例句
EHS management focuses on leading indicators such as training and inspection completion rates.
EHS 管理侧重培训与检查完成率等前置指标。
7
固定搭配
lagging indicator
滞后指标
7
例句
Lagging indicators such as accident rates only reflect past outcomes.
事故率等滞后指标仅反映过往结果。
8
固定搭配
Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR)
可记录事故发生率
8
例句
A rising TRIR signals that proactive safety controls need reinforcement.
TRIR 上升意味着前置安全管控需要加强。
9
固定搭配
Lost Time Injury Rate (LTIR)
误工时工伤事故率
9
例句
LTIR is a key lagging indicator tracked during quarterly business reviews.
LTIR 是季度业务评审中重点跟踪的滞后指标。
10
固定搭配
OSHA compliance review
OSHA 合规审查
10
例句
Full-cycle EHS records must be retained for OSHA compliance review.
全周期 EHS 资料必须留存备查，以满足 OSHA 合规审查要求。
11
固定搭配
qualified contractor directory
合格承包商名录
11
例句
Excellent performers stay on the qualified contractor directory and gain priority for future projects.
绩效优秀的承包商保留在合格承包商名录中，并优先获得后续项目。
12
固定搭配
quarterly business review (QBR)
季度业务评审
12
例句
EHS performance accounts for twenty percent of the QBR total score.
EHS 安全绩效占季度业务评审总分的 20%。
SCENE B

场景 B · 作业完成与绩效评定

1
项目经理 PM
The contractor's construction work is almost finished. What full-site closing safety tasks do we need to arrange first?
承包商施工快要完工了，我们首先要安排哪些全场收尾安全工作？
1
E. H. S. 经理
First, the contractor leads full-site self-inspection. They need to restore site housekeeping, dismantle temporary safety facilities, and walk through the whole area to identify and eliminate potential hazards like leftover flammable materials.
首先由承包商牵头全场自查，现场整洁复原、拆除临时安全设施，全域巡检清理残余的易燃物等隐患。
2
项目经理 PM
After the site cleanup, are there any document requirements we can't ignore?
场地清理完毕后，有没有硬性文件要求不能遗漏？
2
E. H. S. 经理
All high-risk Pre-Task Plans or work permits must be fully closed out. We also need to archive full-cycle EHS records to meet American OSHA review standards, including training records, safety meeting minutes and incident reports.
所有高风险作业前方案、作业许可证必须全部闭环；同时归档全周期 EHS 资料留存备查，满足 OSHA 审查要求，包含培训记录、安全会议纪要、事故报告等。
3
项目经理 PM
Can the contractor move equipment off-site directly once paperwork is finished?
资料做完，承包商就能直接拉设备撤场吗？
3
E. H. S. 经理
No. Our three-party joint safety assessment with the facility department is mandatory. Only if we pass the final site evaluation, they can start the handover and vehicle departure procedures. We also check loading safety of their transport trucks.
不行，必须联合厂务部开展三方安全综合验收。现场评估合格，他们才能办理移交、启动车辆离场流程，我们还要核查运输车辆装载安全。
4
项目经理 PM
After project handover, how do we assess this contractor's safety performance?
项目移交完成后，我们怎么评定这家承包商的安全绩效？
4
E. H. S. 经理
We use the unified evaluation form. Key items cover site housekeeping, PPE compliance, fall protection, chemical management, daily toolbox talks and permit execution, plus injury incident records.
使用统一绩效评估表，核心考核项包含现场整洁、劳保用品佩戴、防坠落、化学品管控、班前会、作业许可执行，同时结合各类工伤事故记录打分。
5
项目经理 PM
Is this performance evaluation only done after project completion? Do we also conduct quarterly reviews?
绩效评估只在项目完工后做吗？平时每个季度要不要考评？
5
E. H. S. 经理
We carry out quarterly EHS performance reviews for all long-term contractors, and conduct full post-project evaluation. Safety performance is a fixed assessment item every quarter.
长期合作承包商每季度都要做 EHS 考评，完工后还要做完整终评，安全表现是季度固定评审项。
6
项目经理 PM
Is the safety score included in their overall quarterly business review rating?
安全得分会计入他们整体季度业务评级吗？
6
E. H. S. 经理
Absolutely. EHS accounts for twenty percent of the total QBR score. Top-rated contractors get priority for new bids, while poor performers will face bidding limits or removal from our contractor list.
当然，EHS 安全占季度综合评审总分的 20%。安全评级优秀的承包商新项目投标优先，表现不达标的会限制投标，甚至移出合格承包商名录。
7
项目经理 PM
When we evaluate contractor performance, what indicators make up the twenty percent EHS weighting?
我们评估承包商绩效时，占比 20% 的 EHS 评分都考核哪些指标？
7
E. H. S. 经理
We prioritize leading indicators like inspections and training completion rates, rather than lagging indicators such as accident rates.
我们重点考核检查和培训完成率这类前置指标，而非事故率等滞后指标。
8
项目经理 PM
Will the final QBR score influence the contractor's service fees?
最终季度评审分数会影响承包商的服务费结算吗？
8
E. H. S. 经理
Absolutely. If points are deducted for the twenty percent EHS section, we will deduct the service fee in the corresponding proportion.
一定的。如果 EHS 这 20% 板块扣分，我们会按对应比例扣除服务费。

We use the unified evaluation form. Key items cover site housekeeping, PPE compliance, fall protection, chemical management, daily toolbox talks and permit execution, plus injury incident records.
PM
Is this performance evaluation only done after project completion? Do we also conduct quarterly reviews?
E. H. S.
We carry out quarterly EHS performance reviews for all long-term contractors, and conduct full post-project evaluation. Safety performance is a fixed assessment item every quarter.
PM
Is the safety score included in their overall quarterly business review rating?
E. H. S.
Absolutely. EHS accounts for twenty percent of the total QBR score. Top-rated contractors get priority for new bids, while poor performers will face bidding limits or removal from our contractor list.
PM
When we evaluate contractor performance, what indicators make up the twenty percent EHS weighting?
E. H. S.
We prioritize leading indicators like inspections and training completion rates, rather than lagging indicators such as accident rates.
PM
Will the final QBR score influence the contractor's service fees?
E. H. S.
Absolutely. If points are deducted for the twenty percent EHS section, we will deduct the service fee in the corresponding proportion.
